Second Look — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026. Second Look is a scam checker built by Muze AI (Chile) for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. You send it a suspicious message, screenshot or voice note, on the web at secondlook.muze.cl or on WhatsApp, and it replies with a verdict.

What we receive

The content you send (text, images or audio) and, on WhatsApp, your phone number and the message ID, as delivered by the WhatsApp Business Platform.

How we use it

Only to produce your answer. The content is sent to Meta's Model API (Muse Spark) to analyze it. If it contains links, we check where they redirect and when their domain was registered (via RDAP). We reply to you on the same channel.

What we keep

We do not store the content you send. Our server logs keep a short one-way hash of your phone number, the type of message and the verdict category, plus the cost of each model call, to operate and debug the service. Web server logs keep visitors' IP addresses, and the daily limit counts requests per IP in memory. We do not sell or share your data and do not use it to train models.

Third parties

Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform and Model API) processes messages under its own terms. Link checks contact the linked site and rdap.org.

Deleting your data

There is no stored content to delete. To ask about the hashed logs or anything else, write to marcochavezrunin@gmail.com.
