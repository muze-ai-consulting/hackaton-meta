## KQ1: YC Requests for Startups (latest lists)

### Takeaway
The latest YC RFS list I verified is Fall 2026: 13 items, shown on the official page with a 2026 copyright footer. Its strongest pattern is AI moving into physical-world operations (field industries, defense, data collection, compute at sea), with agents as the new operating layer (multiplayer sessions, self-maintaining APIs, small-software cloud). The Winter 2027 list was not found.

### Cited Findings
All 13 items below are from the official page https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs (fetched 2026-10-26). Per-item anchors were not identified, so the page URL is given for each.
- The Primer: adaptive AI tutor for reading, writing and arithmetic for young children at consumer scale — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- The Future of American Defense: low-cost interceptors, sensors, drones, logistics and manufacturing for the U.S. Army — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- A Cloud for Small Software: a cloud for deploying and sharing purpose-built tools used by one or a few people — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Multiplayer AI: shared, live AI agent sessions that teams can watch, redirect and hand off together — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Compute at Sea: modular offshore vessels operating together as a global compute cloud — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- AI-Powered Consumer Products for 1 Billion People: mass-market consumer products built on AI agents — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- AI for the Aging Population: voice interfaces, monitoring, home robotics and caregiver coordination for older adults — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- New Operating Systems for the Physical World: software to manage AI agents, robots and human workers together in field industries — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- The Best Time to Build in Crypto: crypto products incl. capital raising, stablecoins, agentic commerce, trading and private blockchains — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Data for the Real World: new ways to collect dense physical-world data to train precise models — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Proving You're Human: privacy-respecting verification that a real human is on the other end of calls, messages and transactions — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- AI-Native Compliance Infrastructure: AI tools consolidating financial compliance work (monitoring, reporting, audit trails) — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Self-Maintaining APIs: agents that detect API provider changes and open pull requests updating customers' codebases — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)

Secondary, not verified on the RFS page:
- Winter 2027 batch: on-time application deadline Nov 2, 2026 at 8 PM PT; early applicants get a decision by Dec 11; batch runs Jan to Mar 2027 in San Francisco — [aggregator](https://www.heysuccess.com/opportunity/Y-Combinator-(winter-2027-batch)-41093). - Excluded (2025): earlier RFS lists such as Summer 2025.

### Inferences
- Physical-world AI is the largest cluster on the Fall 2026 list: items 2, 5, 8, 10 and 7 (field operations, sea compute, data capture, elder-care robotics).
- Agents as shared or self-maintaining infrastructure is a second cluster: items 3, 4 and 13.
- Crypto and "proving you're human" are narrower, less cross-referenced themes.

### Gaps

## KQ3: Meta's stated vision for what developers should build (2026)

### Takeaway
Meta's 2026 developer messaging is about distribution to third-party builders: the Meta Model API (now GA), Muse models, a Muse Connector Platform and Meta AI Connectors so products plug into Meta AI, web and mobile apps for AI glasses, and business agents on WhatsApp. Its hackathon page asks builders to bring AI into real domains and to "give machines new abilities or improve control of physical systems." The July 2025 "personal superintelligence" letter is excluded as a 2025 source.

### Cited Findings
Meta Connect 2026 recap, Meta for Developers, Sep 24, 2026 — https://developers.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-recap/ (unsigned Meta post, fetched 2026-10-26):
- "It's velocity, and how quickly frontier intelligence becomes tools you can build with."
- Stated goal: "Putting intelligence and choice into developer's hands."
- Meta Model API now generally available globally, with enterprise support and compliance.
- Muse Spark 1.3 for complex coding and agentic workflows. Muse Code out of beta and on Windows, "multi-agent by default, allowing parallel workers to write and test code in isolated workspaces."
- Other Muse models: Muse Image; Muse Voice Transcribe (voice-to-text); Muse Glimmer, an open-weights 30B model that runs on a single GPU or Mac Mini.
- Muse Connector Platform: lets developers "connect your products to the everyday tasks people rely on Muse to help out with."
- Wearables Device Access Toolkit 1.0 (glasses): extend mobile apps to display and displayless glasses; camera capture, voice streaming and motion tracking.
- Standard web apps can render on Meta Ray-Ban Display with no native SDK; apps can read device position, location and wrist motion from Meta Neural Band.
- WebMCP (developer preview): lets Meta AI complete tasks in a web app using only functions the developer exposes.
- Meta AI Connectors (developer preview): connect a service via API or MCP server so people can use it inside Meta AI.
- Discovery of glasses experiences on Ray-Ban Display and a curated section of the Meta AI app is "coming soon"; no date given.

Official hackathon page, Meta — https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon (fetched 2026-10-26):
- Headline: "Come and build the future of superintelligence."
- Tracks: Indie developer (judged on "creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works"); Startup (judged on "technical execution and whether it holds up in production"). Each project enters one track.
- Themes: "Build for Your World" (a domain you know firsthand) and "Push the Frontier" (software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials; e.g., "giving a machine a new capability or speeding up a scientific experiment").
- Eligible models and tools: Muse Spark series, Muse Image and SAM 3.1 via the Meta Model API; Meta open-weights models; Muse Code.
- Application review (equal weight): idea clarity, feasibility, theme fit, and technical skill shown.
- Schedule: kickoff Oct 26; workshops and Discord office hours Oct 27 to Nov 4; submissions due Nov 4 at 9 PM ET; judging Nov 4 to 8; closing ceremony and winners Nov 9.
- Prize: $1,000,000 in cash across the two tracks; no per-track or per-place split published.
- Closing call: "Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world."

Meta Business Agent, announced June 3, 2026 (Conversations, London) — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/meta-unveils-ai-business-agent-platform-for-whatsapp-instagram-and-messenger-534739-2026-06-03):
- Business Agent Platform sits on the WhatsApp Business Platform; integrates with Shopify, Zendesk, Shopee. Capabilities: answer business questions, recommend catalogue products, book appointments, qualify leads, hand off to humans, inside the 24-hour customer-service window.
- Access: a third-party guide says the platform is invite-only in select pilot markets and per-token pricing is "in development" — [Omnichat](https://blog.omnichat.ai/meta-business-agent-platform-explained-features-pricing-and-what-the-2026-whatsapp-changes-mean-for-your-business/). Self-serve Business Agent is free for eligible businesses in supported markets.
- India rollout to small businesses reported July 7, 2026 — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/whatsapp-gets-gen-ai-powered-meta-business-agent-for-small-enterprise-owners-in-india-4065355).
- Q1 2026 earnings reportedly showed 10 million weekly conversations between people and business AIs on WhatsApp and Messenger. Only a search summary; primary not fetched.

Muse Spark, April 8, 2026 — first Superintelligence Labs model; runs Meta AI app; US-only at launch; roughly on par with leaders on language and visual understanding, trails on coding and abstract reasoning, tied for fourth on Artificial Analysis index — [Neos Kosmos, Apr 9, 2026](https://neoskosmos.com/en/2026/04/09/life/technology/meta-unveils-first-ai-model-from-superintelligence-team/).

## KQ4: Themes that converge across lists (2026)

### Takeaway
Two themes appear across YC Fall 2026, Greylock, Lightspeed and Meta's 2026 messaging: physical-world AI operations, and agents as a shared, maintained operating layer. Those are the themes investor-minded judges are most likely to recognize.

### Cited Findings
- Physical-world operations (agents + robots + people in field industries, plus physical data): YC items "New Operating Systems for the Physical World" and "Data for the Real World" ([YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)); Lightspeed-led Kyber seed (June 2026, source summary); Q2 2026 robotics concentration ([CrowdFund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/07/293270-physical-ai-surges-to-the-forefront-of-q2-2026-venture-activity/)); Meta hackathon "Push the Frontier" theme naming robotics, manufacturing, energy and materials ([Meta hackathon page](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon)).
- Agents as shared or maintained infrastructure: YC "Multiplayer AI" and "Self-Maintaining APIs" ([YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)); Greylock's long-running agents as the application layer ([StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/news/greylock-bets-1-5b-on-the-next-ai-wave)); Meta Muse Code "multi-agent by default" and Meta AI Connectors ([Meta Connect recap](https://developers.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-recap/)).
- Consumer and business agents at scale: YC "AI-Powered Consumer Products for 1 Billion People" ([YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)); Meta Business Agent ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/meta-unveils-ai-business-agent-platform-for-whatsapp-instagram-and-messenger-534739-2026-06-03)); Sequoia's services framing (secondhand).
- Single-source only: verification ("Proving You're Human") and compliance infrastructure (YC only); elder care (YC only); crypto (YC only).

### Inferences
- Strongest convergence: physical-world operations with agents coordinating humans and machines. It shows up in YC, in VC capital flows, and in Meta's own theme text.
- Weakest convergence: crypto and identity verification, which appear on one list each.

### Gaps
- Convergence is based on about four sources, one of them a YC page and one a Meta page; it is not a survey of all investors.

## KQ5: Underserved themes buildable as a convincing 9-day demo

### Takeaway
Two candidates fit the hackathon themes and the stack (multimodal LLM, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe, tool calling). (A) a field-operations copilot for a physical site, and (B) a physical-world data-capture pipeline. Both can be scoped to a single domain the team knows firsthand, as the "Build for Your World" theme asks. Funding density was not verified for any of these; the "underserved" judgement is my inference from the lists above.

### Cited Findings
Candidate map (inferences, not sourced market claims):
- A. Field-operations copilot (phone or glasses capture to structured action). Multimodal LLM reads the equipment or gauge state; SAM 3.1 segments components or defects; Muse Voice Transcribe takes spoken shift notes; a tool-calling agent opens a work order or checklist and writes a compliance log. Maps to YC "New Operating Systems for the Physical World" and to "Push the Frontier." Demo: a 2-minute phone capture on a real object the team can access. Risk: "production readiness" needs realistic data; scope tightly.
- B. Physical-world data capture (YC "Data for the Real World"): record a manual process on phone; SAM 3.1 tracks objects and hands; the model writes an event dataset with labels, exportable for training. Strong and mechanical demo; the business case needs a chosen vertical.
- C. Self-maintaining integration agent (YC "Self-Maintaining APIs" and "A Cloud for Small Software"): watch an API changelog or spec diff; Muse Code opens a PR updating client code; tests run in CI. Easy to show. Developer-tools space is probably crowded; not verified.
- D. Vertical WhatsApp agent on Meta Business Agent: Meta's own product area, but access is invite-only per third party, and WhatsApp BSP vendors are already visible in the sources. Lower odds.
- E. Glasses assistant for technicians (Wearables Device Access Toolkit, web apps for Ray-Ban Display): strong Meta alignment, but judges will likely not have glasses, and the toolkit is version 1.0. Medium-high risk for 9 days.
- F. Voice-call human verification ("Proving You're Human"): hard to make reliable, and synthetic-voice detection is a moving target. Avoid for this window.
- G. AI for elder care: clinical and regulatory exposure; avoid for this window.

### Inferences
- Best fit for "investor brains" plus "production readiness": A, scoped to one domain the team knows, with B's capture pipeline as the data layer. This hits YC's physical-world cluster and Meta's Push the Frontier theme.
- Avoid model-dependent claims about 1M-token context; design around a bounded context.
- Avoid hardware dependency for judging; a phone demo on real objects is safer.

### Gaps
- Funding density and competitor count for A and B not verified.
- What "production readiness" means for this judging criterion was not defined by Meta beyond "holds up in production."
- No past hackathon winners reviewed.
