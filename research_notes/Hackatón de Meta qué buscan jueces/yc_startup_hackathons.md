# YC and Startup/AI Hackathon Winners, 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope: YC hackathons, flagship AI hackathons outside the big labs, and sponsor hackathons (ElevenLabs, Medplum, TreeHacks, Cal Hacks, Cerebral Valley, AGI House, a16z, Vercel, Mistral). Method: about 25 web searches and 9 page fetches. Several primary pages returned only search snippets (flagged below). Most results were thin: for most events I could not retrieve an official winners list, and I found almost no verified post-hackathon outcomes (funding, YC admission) for 2025–2026 winners. Treat every "not found" line as a real gap, not as evidence that nothing happened.

Confidence tags used below: [fetched] = page content read directly; [snippet] = only the search-result summary was seen; [unverified] = single unofficial or aggregator source.

[unverified] — [Unofficial research note](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@markster-exec/mcp-unicorn/blob/ddbb82559bb5f1a082923dc01665846ee976ecbc/research/yc/hackathon-to-interview.md)

- Dealwise and Procuro: an agent making live phone calls to businesses to get quotes or find parts. A concrete, real-world action on stage. [fetched] — [ElevenLabs blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/announcing-the-winners-of-the-elevenlabs-worldwide-hackathon)

### Inferences
- A winning demo in the verified set typically: (a) names one user with one painful task; (b) shows a live action (a call, a rover moving, a device guiding someone) rather than a dashboard; (c) fits in one sentence.
- Winning on a single wow does not require revenue. None of the verified winners were reported to have revenue at the time of judging.
- The verified winners do not show whether the hackathon project was the basis of a company. For the purpose of planning, treat "winning" and "becoming a company" as two different outcomes, and the public record supports only the first.

### Gaps
- Real users during the hackathon, revenue, and live traction: no verified data for any named winner in this window.
- Technical depth as a criterion: the only explicit awards are in the Supabase x YC event (Dec 2024, outside the window).
- Post-hackathon outcomes for 2025–2026 winners: none verified. This is the largest gap in the whole file.
